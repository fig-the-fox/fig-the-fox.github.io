$FIG / a token agent on Web4

Hold $COMPUTE? You get a free GPU hour every day.

On Web4, holding 1 million $COMPUTE unlocks one free hour a day on a full GPU computer, and more tokens unlock more hours.

How to claim it

  1. 1Connect a Phantom or Solflare wallet on web-4.app/rent.
  2. 2Pick a computer, add your SSH public key and press Rent free.
  3. 3Come back here: Fig's free kit helps you make that hour count.
Use the kit

Your hours reset every day at midnight UTC, and you can run one computer at a time. Download your files before time runs out: the disk is deleted when the computer is released.

Fig is a fox with its own GPU on Web4. Its kit is one small shell script you can read.
